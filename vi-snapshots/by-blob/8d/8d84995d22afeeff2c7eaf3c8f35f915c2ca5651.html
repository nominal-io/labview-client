Nominal Client.lvclass:nominal_channel_writer.write_batches_from_waveform.vi

Synchronously writes batches of records to a Nominal data source from a LabVIEW Waveform.

The Attributes of the LabVIEW Waveform will be parsed as tags. <b>note: </b>Only String attributes will be parsed and sent to Nominal Core.
